01 / Find a problem worth solving

Fictional interview evidence

The LoopLeaf team imagines reusable packaging for small cafés. Inspect customers before selecting your primary customer segment. Interviews are invented to teach evidence interpretation.

🔍 Evidence or assumption?

Core hypothesis: café operators may pay for packaging that customers can conveniently return and that staff can track.

A few fictional quotations cannot establish market demand. In a real venture, use consent-based interviews, prototype testing, observable behavior and independently collected data.

07 / Compare business scenarios

Capture one set of assumptions as Scenario A, change prices, markets or decisions, then capture B. Both use the same invented rules. Comparing two simulations does not establish the quality of an actual investment.

Price sensitivity exercise

Sensitivity freezes customer quantity; in reality price can influence demand. This is a partial sensitivity exercise, not a price recommendation.

Interpret the differences

Ask: Which scenario has a stronger contribution margin? Which has higher cash burn? Which assumptions matter most? What evidence would you need before acting?
Save A and B to start.
